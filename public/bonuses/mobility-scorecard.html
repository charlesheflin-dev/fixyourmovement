Your Mobility Scorecard

Weeks 5–8 — Phase 2 of Your Recovery
You've made it through Phase 1 — and if you've been tracking, you've already seen your pain start to drop. Now it's time to build strength. In Phase 2, you're going to start doing calf raises, and this scorecard tracks two simple things: your pain score and how many calf raises you can do. As the weeks go by, you'll see your pain keep dropping and your calf raise count keep climbing. That crossover is the moment everything clicks.

How to Use This Scorecard

Do your Phase 2 exercises

Follow along with the video modules for Weeks 5–8.

Same 0–10 scale you've been using. How does your foot feel today?

Count your calf raises

How many calf raises could you complete today? Write the number.

Two numbers. That's all. Pain going down, calf raises going up. Let's watch it happen.
