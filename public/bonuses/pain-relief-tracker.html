Your Pain Relief Tracker

Weeks 1–4 — Phase 1 of Your Recovery
Welcome to Phase 1. Over the next 4 weeks, you're going to watch your pain shrink — and this tracker is how you'll see it happen. After you finish each calm-the-tissue video module, come here and log two things: your pain level and what you did. That's it. No complicated forms. No guesswork. Just a quick check-in so you can look back and see how far you've come.

How to Use This Tracker

Watch your video module

Complete the calm-the-tissue exercises from the course.

Rate your pain

On a scale of 0 to 10, how does your foot feel right now? (0 = no pain, 10 = worst pain imaginable)

Check what you did

Check off which calm-the-tissue exercises you completed today.

Do this after every session. In 4 weeks, you'll see the trend — and you'll be amazed.

Understanding Your Pain Scale

0
No pain at all
1-2
Barely noticeable — you have to think about it to feel it
3-4
Mild — it's there, but it doesn't stop you from doing things
5-6
Moderate — it's hard to ignore and starts affecting your day
7-8
Severe — it's difficult to do normal activities
9-10
Worst pain imaginable — you can't function
