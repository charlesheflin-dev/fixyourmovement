Your Daily Habit Builder

Weeks 9–12 — Phase 3 of Your Recovery
You've crushed it. Your pain is down. Your strength is up. Now let's make sure it stays that way — for good. Phase 3 is about building tiny habits into your daily routine so your feet stay strong without you even thinking about it. No extra gym time. No separate exercise sessions. Just small movements attached to things you're already doing every single day.

Your 4 Daily Habits

Each of these takes less than 2 minutes. You don't need to set aside special time — you're stacking them onto things you already do.
☕
Toe Spreads While Making Coffee
Morning routine
While your coffee brews (or your tea steeps), stand at the counter and do toe spreads. Spread your toes as wide as you can, hold for 2 seconds, release. Repeat. Write down how many you did.
💻
Seated Foot Exercises at Your Desk
During work/daytime
While sitting at your desk (or kitchen table, or couch — wherever you sit during the day), do your seated foot exercises. Write down how many you completed.
🌅
AM Calf Raises While Brushing Teeth
Morning routine
While brushing your teeth in the morning, do calf raises. Rise up on your toes, hold for a second, lower down. Write down how many you did before you finished brushing.
🌙
PM Calf Raises While Brushing Teeth
Evening routine
Same thing, evening. Brushing teeth = calf raises. Write down your count before you finish brushing at night.
