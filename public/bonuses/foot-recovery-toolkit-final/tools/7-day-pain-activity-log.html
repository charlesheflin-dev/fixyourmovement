📋 How to Use This Log

  • Step 1: In the "My Activity Baseline" section, check the activities you typically do each week. These will carry over to each day.
  • Step 2: Each day, check which baseline activities you actually completed.
  • Step 3: Record your pain levels (0-10) at morning, midday, and evening.
  • Step 4: Add any notes about new activities, interventions tried, or patterns noticed.
  • Step 5: At the end of the week, complete the Weekly Summary section.
