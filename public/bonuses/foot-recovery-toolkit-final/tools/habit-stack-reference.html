The Habit Stack

Anchoring Micro-Habits into Your Existing Routines

🧠 The Science of the Stack

A Habit Stack uses an existing "Anchor" (an automatic daily behavior) to trigger your new "Micro-Habit." This bypasses the need for willpower.

"After I [Current Habit], I will [Recovery Move]."

☕ Your Built-In Anchors

Use habits you already do every day as triggers for your recovery moves.

☕

Morning Coffee Stack

"While the coffee brews, I will do 10 Toe Spreads."

💻

Work-From-Home Stack

"Every time I sit at my desk, I will do 5 Short Foot ISOs."

🌙

Bedtime Stack

"While I brush my teeth, I will do a 60-second Calf Stretch."

⚡ The One-Rep Rule (Fallback Plan)

If you are too busy for a full session, do one single rep of your stack. The goal isn't just physical—it's neurological. Doing one rep keeps the "Consistency Chain" alive in your brain. One rep is a victory; zero reps is a break in the system.

📝 Key Points

  • Start small: Choose just 1-2 habit stacks to begin
  • Be specific: "After I pour coffee" is better than "In the morning"
  • Make it easy: Your recovery move should take < 2 minutes
  • Track it: Put a checkmark on your calendar each day you complete your stack
  • Stack on success: Once one stack is automatic, add another
