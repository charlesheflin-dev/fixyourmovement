🚦 Color Code Definitions

Each day, assess how you feel and assign a color. This determines your activity level for that day.

GREEN DAY

Feel Great — No symptoms, full energy

Action: Add 10% to last week's load. This is your progression day.

YELLOW DAY

Mild Twinges — Slight discomfort, caution needed

Action: Stay at current load. Don't increase, don't decrease.

RED DAY

Flare-up — Pain >4/10 or significant symptoms

Action: Reduce load by 20%. Focus on recovery activities only.

⭐ THE GOLDEN RULE

After a RED day, schedule a GREEN day

Never stack two high-intensity days back-to-back without recovery

⏰ Recovery Window Rules

After each intensity level, you need a specific recovery window before your next session.

Intensity 2 Allow 6-8 hours before another moderate session
Intensity 3 Allow 24 hours before another intensity 3+ session
Intensity 4+ Allow 48-72 hours with structured recovery

💡 Pro Tips

  • Plan your week on Sunday: Look at your schedule and assign colors before the week starts
  • Be flexible: If you wake up feeling different than planned, adjust your color code
  • Track patterns: Notice which activities consistently lead to yellow or red days
  • Celebrate green days: Use them to progress, but don't rush the process
