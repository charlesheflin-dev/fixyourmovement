Progressive Foot Strength Progress Log

Objective Metrics for Intrinsic Muscle Development

📋 How to Use This Log

  • Form is a Signal: If technique breaks down, that's a sign you're moving beyond your tissues' abilities and need to rest/stop.
  • Track every session: Record exercises, reps, load, and RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion).
  • Use the progress rules: Follow the criteria to know when to increase, maintain, or decrease load.
  • Focus on control: Technique should be considered, but control is what matters most.

🎯 Form is a Signal

Most people don't have a clue as to what they are and aren't doing wrong. That's okay — focus on what you CAN feel.

"If your technique and form begin to break down, you are likely moving beyond your tissues' abilities and need to rest/stop."

📊 RPE Scale (Rate of Perceived Exertion)

Use this 1-10 scale to rate how hard each exercise feels. This helps determine when to progress.

💡 Technique vs. Control

While technique is important for getting proper tension on the proper tissues, research doesn't always support "ugly" movement being worse for you. Technique should be considered, but not harped on. Control is what matters most. Most people won't have perfect control out of the gate — that's normal.

📈 Progress Rules

Use these criteria to determine when to adjust your load.

INCREASE
If: Completed all targets with RPE ≤ 6/10 for 2 consecutive sessions AND no pain increase
Then: +10% weight or +2-4 reps
KEEP SAME
If: Met targets but RPE 7-8/10 or mild delayed ache
Then: Keep load, focus on form and control
MODIFY
If: Pain DURING exercise > 3/10 OR sharp/radiating/tingling/swelling
Then: Stop, reduce load 30-50%, contact mentor if persists
